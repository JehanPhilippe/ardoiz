# Design System — CapDevoirs (DA 3 : Manuel Illustré & Index Latéral)

Adopted from Impeccable Direction Roll (`seed:40cc61a5b0e2f772`, Challenger `rw-lexicon-shoulder-and-plate`) and combined with `ui-ux-pro-max` WCAG AA accessibility rules.

## Surface Mode & Visual World
- **Mode**: Operate (daily school homework, exercises, method sheets, quizzes, official Éduscol curriculum, and multi-year archive).
- **Architecture**: Running guide-word header (`.guide-header`) + right-edge notched **Thumb-Index Shoulder** (`.lexicon-shoulder`) switching school-year volumes (`CE2`, `CM1`, `CM2`, `6e`, and the `1er Mars N+1` gate tab).
- **Signature Move**: **Table des Matières en Chiffres Romains & Planches Pédagogiques Encadrées** — black engraving typography on uncoated ivory paper (`#FAF7F0`) with every vivid color quarantined inside 2px-bordered Study Plates (`.study-plate`: Cobalt `#1D4ED8`, Emerald `#15803D`, Vermilion `#B91C1C`, Ochre `#B45309`).

## Typography
- **Display**: `Bitter` (`600`, `700`, `800`), slab-serif textbook authority.
- **Body**: `Source Sans 3` (`400`, `600`, `700`, `800`), crisp reading fluency with `tabular-nums` for `/20` scores and dates.
